## What to look at

- In `tool.risk` the first threshold that is met wins, and `deny` comes before `ask`. **One** risk is enough to decide: raise just one and watch the verdict change.
- In `agent.wakeup` the intervals are half-open: a probability equal to the threshold falls in the upper band. Set 0.30 and 0.30: it no longer sleeps.
- The model never changed. Your policy did, and with it how many decisions go to a person.

The values in the included flows (0.55 and 0.85; 0.3 and 0.5) are a first approximation. To pin them with your data, see the [decision guide](/en/guia-decisiones/) and the [exercises](/en/ejercicios/).
